TROOPER BACKGROUND

Darren Vaust, designated GC-3170, is an enlistee of Corellian origin.

Prior to enlistment, he worked as a scrapper and restorer of small-scale reactors and other (mostly antiquated) energy systems in and around Coronet City. He found moderate success in his endeavors for just under four years, spanning his late teens into young adulthood, until running afoul of the now-defunct Coronet Isotope & Engineering (CIE), which officially registered him as a debtor - What magnitude of debt was owed, if any, was been lost in records mismanagement.

Regardless, he fled to Gus Treta, a large orbital station and mining hub in the same system, where he maintained and occasionally operated small-scale fusion reactors on their localized grid. Unable to afford travel outside of CEC's reach, he operated under a false identity.

After his 36-month contract expired, he enlisted to offer his technical skills to the Empire—or so he claimed. It is far more likely that he was simply fleeing his debt. While this was discovered during pre-enlistment background scraping, the nature of the debt was deemed 'indeterminate' and therefore archived without action.

Following his enlistment, he was stationed on Lothal as a Garrison Power Technician at the Sienar Fleet Systems Factory (which absorbed CIE), managing (in part) their power grid for just over three years. Shortly before his service term ended, he applied for the Janus Program and was selected for transfer to Felucia.

Because his prior enlistment was Naval, this transfer required a service waiver and redesignation to 327th GC. Waiver authorized to fill a critical need for experienced technicians on Felucia, where grid systems are notoriously vulnerable.
